  preferred channel because drivers need no app/login; a named small carrier
  (DFH Transportation) saw response rate jump from **71% (phone+email

## Winner — SMSDispatch · verdict: BUILD ✅ (composite 7.28, all 6 gates passed)

**Concept:** SMS text-back for small trucking carriers — dispatchers push load
assignments, pickup/delivery details, and route updates by text instead of
phone/radio; drivers confirm without an app or login. Usage-based pricing
(per-message or per-driver) fits the fragmented, mostly-under-10-truck buyer
base better than a flat subscription.

**This is the THIRD build-gate winner and the SECOND time SMS has beaten a
voice-AI version of the same market/pain pair**, this time by a wider margin:
SMSDispatch (7.28) vs. 2026-08-01's DispatchCatch, same trucking-dispatch pair
via voice AI (6.60) — a **+0.68** gap, larger than TextBack Local's +0.32 edge
over CallCatch Local in trades. The pattern is no longer a hypothesis.
