## Evidence Notes (sourced 2026-08-01)

- **A (TrustBadge Home):** consumer demand for verified-pro trust signals is
  strong — **60% will pay extra for enhanced background checks**, **86% say a
  platform's safety reputation matters to their choice**, **58% of gig-platform
  users want worker background checks**
  ([bizwire/Truas survey](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/bizwire-2025-2-11-verified-trust-is-the-new-currency-truas-national-consumer-survey-highlights-growing-concerns-over-security-and-transparency-in-digital-platforms),
  [rigbyjs.com](https://www.rigbyjs.com/blog/services-marketplace-features)).
  Counter-signal: this is a trust *layer*, not a standalone product — hard to
  sell independent of a booking platform; ease scored low (integration burden).
- **B (DispatchCatch):** the strongest evidence pull of the run. A small
  fleet misses **8+ bookable calls/night** at ~$45 avg value; **10-20 truck
  carriers lose $35,000–$75,000/yr** in missed night/weekend calls alone; full
  manual-dispatch cost for a 20-truck carrier runs **$127,000–$305,000/yr**
  ([virtualnexgen.com](https://virtualnexgen.com/blog/trucking-company-missed-calls-cost),
  [pr.com/TransportBPO](https://www.pr.com/press-release/973709),
  [numeo.ai](https://numeo.ai/blog/the-real-cost-of-manual-dispatch-time-loads-and-revenue-you-re-losing)).
  Counter-signal: buyer sophistication and existing dispatch-software
  incumbents (DispatchMVP etc.) push ease down; usage-based pricing adds
  revenue-forecast uncertainty (kept confidence low on ease).
- **C (PawCompliant):** no new pricing evidence found; carries forward the
  weak profit case from 2026-07-30's compliance-training candidates (commodity
  risk, per-seat cost still TBD).
- **D (CommunityLine):** buyer is a community org, not a paying individual —
