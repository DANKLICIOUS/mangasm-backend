## Candidate Scorecards

### A — VenueText

**What it does:** Missed-call text-back and booking confirmation reminder SMS for independent wedding and event venues (barn venues, estate venues, boutique banquet halls; 50-300 guest capacity, owner-operated or small staff).

**Evidence:**
- Wedding venue inquiries are high-intent, high-urgency leads — couples contact 5-10 venues and book the first one that responds; a missed call at a venue is frequently a lost booking worth $3,000-15,000.
- Independent venues (not hotel ballrooms or restaurant private rooms) typically rely on a single coordinator or owner answering the phone; after-hours and weekend missed calls are common.
- WeddingWire/The Knot marketplace handles listing and inquiry routing but does not provide SMS missed-call text-back after the initial platform message — standalone SMS fill-in for phone inquiries is unaddressed.
- Reachability: venue coordinator Facebook groups (Wedding Venue Owners group), bridal association networks (NACE, ILEA), and The Knot Pro vendor community — active peer-to-peer channels.
- Booking confirmation reminders address a distinct second pain: couples who book 12-18 months out miss contract milestones and deposit deadlines — a reminder sequence that mirrors the cleaning/vet reminder pattern is directly applicable.
- TCPA: standard opt-in at inquiry form; no industry-specific regulatory overlay.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **8.0** — high confidence. A single recovered booking inquiry ($5,000+ average venue fee) makes $99/mo look trivial; strong ROI narrative. Market: 10,000+ independent wedding venues in the US. No dominant SMS-specific missed-call tool in the niche — WeddingWire/Knot are lead-gen platforms, not post-call SMS tools.
- Popularity Potential: **7.5** — medium confidence. Venue owners are organized and reachable via industry associations and Facebook groups; The Knot Pro and WeddingWire vendor communities are warm channels for peer referrals. Wedding industry is relationship-driven — word-of-mouth among venue coordinators is strong.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **8.0** — high confidence. Same dual-trigger architecture as VetText and CleanText — missed_call trigger + multi-touch reminder sequence (booking milestone reminders at 90/30/7 days); keyword set CONFIRM/C/YES, CANCEL/R/NO, STOP; no venue software API needed for MVP (manual booking entry); onboard under 10 minutes.

**Composite:** (8.0 × 0.40) + (7.5 × 0.35) + (8.0 × 0.25) = 3.20 + 2.625 + 2.00 = **7.825**

**Build gate check:**
- Composite ≥ 7.0: ✅ (7.825)

### B — ShootText

**What it does:** Inquiry follow-up text-back and session reminder SMS for independent wedding photographers and videographers.

**Evidence:**
- Wedding photographers receive high volumes of initial inquiries (email and phone) but are single operators — calls during shoots go unanswered routinely. A missed inquiry call from a couple is a missed $2,000-5,000 booking.
- Honeybook and Dubsado are the dominant CRM/workflow tools for wedding photographers — both include automated email follow-up sequences; SMS is not a native feature in either at the entry pricing tier.
- The photographer market is large (200,000+ working wedding photographers in the US) but highly fragmented; the buyer profile is a solo creative professional, not a business operator — willingness to pay for a business tool is lower and more variable than venue owners.
- Reachability: photography Facebook groups (The Photo Forum, Photography Business groups) are very active; Instagram is a primary channel but not ideal for B2B tool promotion.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **7.0** — medium confidence. $79/mo is defensible; ROI case is real (single recovered inquiry covers months of subscription). Solo creative professional buyer is price-sensitive compared to venue owner.
- Popularity Potential: **7.0** — medium confidence. Large, active, reachable community; Honeybook/Dubsado email automation partial incumbent but SMS gap is real.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **8.5** — high confidence. Simplest possible architecture — single missed-call trigger for MVP; reminder sequence for contracted sessions in phase 2.

### C — CaterText

**What it does:** Inquiry follow-up and event confirmation reminder SMS for independent catering companies (10-50 employees, corporate and social events).

**Evidence:**
- Catering inquiry-to-booking flow is complex: initial inquiry → tasting → proposal → contract → deposit — multiple touchpoints spread over weeks or months. An SMS missed-call text-back only addresses the first step; the rest require workflow automation beyond MVP scope.
- Independent caterers rely on event coordinators (venue, hotel, corporate) for a significant portion of referrals — the direct inquiry phone call is one channel among many, not the primary bottleneck.
- Incumbent tools: Caterease, Total Party Planner (TPP) — purpose-built catering management software that includes proposal, invoice, and client communication workflows; established operators are already tool-heavy.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **7.0** — medium confidence. High-ticket events make revenue recovery case viable; but complex multi-step sales cycle means SMS alone doesn't close a booking.
- Popularity Potential: **6.5** — medium confidence. Caterers are reachable via NACE and catering association channels; but sales cycle complexity makes the product feel incomplete without deeper workflow integration.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **6.0** — medium confidence. Missed-call text-back is simple; but the value proposition weakens fast as the buyer realizes they need more than one SMS to convert a catering inquiry.

**Composite:** (7.0 × 0.40) + (6.5 × 0.35) + (6.0 × 0.25) = 2.80 + 2.275 + 1.50 = **6.575**

**Build gate check:**
- Composite ≥ 7.0: ❌ (6.575)
- **GATE: FAILED ❌** — multi-step sales cycle limits SMS value to one touchpoint; incumbent catering software penetration high.

### D — DJText

**What it does:** Inquiry follow-up and event reminder SMS for mobile DJs and entertainment companies.

**Evidence:**
- Mobile DJ market is highly fragmented (solo operators and small 2-5 person companies); similar inquiry pattern to photographers — missed calls during events are common.

- GigSalad and The Bash (formerly GigMasters) are dominant marketplace platforms for DJ booking — both handle inquiry routing natively, reducing the unaddressed-phone-inquiry problem for platform-sourced leads.

- Profit Potential: **6.5** — medium confidence. Lower average booking value vs. venue; GigSalad/Bash coverage reduces the unaddressed-inquiry problem for many DJs.
- Popularity Potential: **6.5** — medium confidence. Active DJ community (Mobile Beat, DJ community Facebook groups); but marketplace platform penetration means many DJs already have inquiry routing covered.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **8.0** — high confidence. Simple missed-call text-back architecture; identical to existing engine.

**Composite:** (6.5 × 0.40) + (6.5 × 0.35) + (8.0 × 0.25) = 2.60 + 2.275 + 2.00 = **6.875**

**Build gate check:**
- Composite ≥ 7.0: ❌ (6.875 — near-miss)
- **GATE: FAILED ❌** — lower ticket value weakens ROI; marketplace platform coverage reduces addressable inquiry volume.

## Decision

**Winner:** VenueText (Missed-Call Text-Back + Booking Milestone Reminders for Independent Wedding/Event Venues)  
**Composite Score:** 7.825  
**Decision:** BUILD ✅ — 13th build-gate winner  
**Why it won:** VenueText has the highest revenue-recovery-per-event of any vertical tested to date — a single recovered $5,000+ venue booking inquiry makes the ROI story immediate and visceral. Independent venue owners are a clearly identifiable, reachable buyer with active peer communities. Architecture adds one new pattern beyond existing verticals: multi-milestone booking reminders (90/30/7 days before event), which can be implemented as a configurable reminder chain on the existing engine without new code.

**Strongest unresolved assumption:** Inquiry channel mix — wedding couples increasingly initiate contact via The Knot/WeddingWire message thread rather than phone call; if phone-based missed-call text-back covers only 30-40% of venue inquiries (vs. 70-80% for verticals like vet clinics or grooming), the TAM per account shrinks. Needs field verification before outreach: ask 10 venue owners what percentage of new inquiries come via phone vs. platform message.

**ShootText note:** Second-place at 7.375, also passes the build gate. Solo creative professional buyer is more price-sensitive than venue owner; worth packaging after VenueText validates.
