## Candidate Scorecards

### A — PitchVoice

**What it does:** AI voice receptionist for independent real estate agents — answers inbound buyer and seller inquiry calls 24/7 using a conversational AI agent, qualifies the caller (buying timeline, price range, property type), books a showing or callback appointment, and sends the agent an SMS summary + sends the caller an SMS confirmation. Missed call from a prospective buyer or seller triggers the AI voice agent; no live agent required during showings or evenings.

**Evidence:**
- Independent real estate agents (1.5M licensed agents in the US; 87% independent contractors or small-brokerage affiliated) miss inquiry calls constantly — they are often in showings, driving, or unavailable. Each missed call from a buyer or seller is a lead worth $5,000–30,000 in potential commission.
- Current tooling: most agents use voicemail. CRM platforms (Follow Up Boss, Sierra Interactive) have AI features but no purpose-built AI voice receptionist as a standalone SMS-anchored product.
- Pain specificity: agents lose an estimated 27% of inbound calls to voicemail with no callback — at median commission of $9,000/transaction, one recovered lead per month is worth $108,000/year. $199/mo subscription is trivially cheap by comparison.
- Market: 1.5M licensed agents; NAR membership ($150/yr) includes a searchable directory; state association directories and Realtor boards are organized and reachable; Instagram/TikTok real estate creator communities are massive and referral-active.
- TCPA: inbound calls from prospects who dialed the agent's number = implied consent for the AI to answer on the agent's behalf (same as a human receptionist). Caller disclosure ("You've reached [Agent Name]'s AI assistant") satisfies transparency.
- Dual trigger fit: (1) missed_call from inbound prospect → AI voice answers within 1 ring; (2) reminder_due for follow-up call or showing confirmation → SMS to prospect.
- AI-native alignment: ✅ full — AI voice is the core product experience, not an add-on.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **8.0** — high confidence. $199/mo for a solo agent is trivial vs. one recovered lead ($5,000+ commission). 1.5M addressable buyers. NAR directory provides organized, low-CAC reachability. Competition in purpose-built "AI voice receptionist for real estate agents" is nascent.
- Popularity Potential: **8.0** — high confidence. Real estate agents are among the most active professional communities on social media and in peer referral networks (BiggerPockets, ActiveRain, Facebook groups, Instagram creators). "AI that answers your calls and books showings while you're in a showing" is an extremely viral story.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **7.0** — high confidence. Voice AI setup requires script configuration (caller qualification flow, showing booking integration with agent's calendar); more steps than pure-SMS onboarding but still achievable in under 30 minutes with a guided setup flow. TCPA disclosure is built into the first greeting.

**Composite:** (8.0 × 0.40) + (8.0 × 0.35) + (7.0 × 0.25) = 3.200 + 2.800 + 1.750 = **7.750**

**Build gate check:**

- ≥ 2 medium/high confidence: ✅ (Profit high, Popularity high, Ease high)

### B — VoiceBack

**What it does:** AI voice receptionist for independent home service contractors (plumbers, HVAC technicians, electricians, roofers) — answers missed inbound calls from homeowners requesting service quotes or emergency dispatch. AI voice captures job type, address, and urgency; sends the contractor an SMS summary; sends the homeowner an SMS confirmation that their request was received with an ETA for callback.

*Note: VoiceBack is the voice AI tier above TextBack Local (SMS-only, already launched). TextBack Local sends an SMS text-back when a call is missed. VoiceBack answers the call with voice AI and has a conversation. Different product, different price point, potentially a TextBack Local upgrade path.*

**Evidence:**
- Home service contractors (plumbers, HVAC, electricians) are the original TextBack Local market. Validated: they miss calls, those missed calls cost jobs. TextBack Local sends an SMS after the missed call; VoiceBack answers the call and has a live AI conversation.
- The upgrade case: a homeowner calling about a burst pipe wants immediate acknowledgment — "Hi, this is Jake's Plumbing AI assistant, I can take your emergency info right now and dispatch Jake" is more reassuring than an SMS text-back 10 seconds after the call drops.
- Market: same 300,000+ independent contractor buyers as TextBack Local. VoiceBack targets the segment willing to pay $199/mo vs. $99/mo for a richer product.
- Portfolio synergy: VoiceBack and TextBack Local can co-exist as good/better tiers. VoiceBack isn't a competitor to TextBack Local — it's the next step up.
- Reachability: same channels as TextBack Local — contractor Facebook groups, ServiceTitan/Jobber user communities, PHCC (Plumbing-Heating-Cooling Contractors Association), NECA (electrical contractors) directories.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **7.5** — medium/high confidence. $199/mo vs. $99/mo for TextBack Local; same buyer with proven pain and proven willingness-to-pay (TextBack Local already launched). 300,000+ contractors. Voice AI upgrade path is a natural upsell story.
- Popularity Potential: **7.5** — medium/high confidence. Same strong word-of-mouth channels as TextBack Local. "AI that actually ANSWERS your calls, not just texts back" is a more compelling story than SMS alone. Existing TextBack Local customers are pre-qualified referral sources.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **7.0** — high confidence. Similar to PitchVoice but simpler script (job type + address + urgency, no calendar booking); contractor workflows are familiar from TextBack Local validation; 20–30 min setup with guided flow.

### C — VocalText

**What it does:** AI voice outbound call to family members of home care agency clients — a daily "good morning" AI voice call from the agency to the client's designated family contact ("Hi, this is Sunshine Home Care calling with an update on your mother — she had breakfast, completed her morning routine, and is doing well today"). SMS fallback if the call goes unanswered. Missed inquiry from a family researching home care → immediate SMS follow-up.

*Note: VocalText is the third home care adjacent product in two days (after CareText for scheduling, WatchText for client wellness check-in). The buyer is the same home care agency owner, but the use case is distinct — this is family engagement and peace-of-mind, not client safety monitoring.*

**Evidence:**
- Home care agencies compete on trust and family confidence. Families who feel informed and reassured are less likely to switch agencies; families who feel disconnected generate complaint calls and churn.
- Pain: calling each family member daily is impossibly time-consuming; most agencies send a weekly email at best. AI voice call to the family is a differentiator agencies can advertise ("We call your family every morning").
- Market: 33,000+ licensed home health and companion care agencies — same buyer as CareText and WatchText, but this is now the third product targeting the same buyer vertical.
- Portfolio saturation risk: CareText, WatchText, TrustText, and VocalText all share the home care agency as a buyer. Multiple products for the same buyer is fine for a portfolio, but may dilute go-to-market focus.
- AI voice outbound to family members: TCPA requires prior express consent for AI-initiated outbound calls. Family members must opt in at intake. This is manageable (included in care agreement) but adds compliance friction vs. inbound AI calls.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**

- Popularity Potential: **7.0** — medium confidence. "AI calls your family every morning" is emotionally compelling and shareable. NAHC, HCA channels — same organized reachability as WatchText/CareText.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **7.0** — high confidence. Daily outbound call = scheduled trigger; SMS fallback = standard missed_call pattern; family opt-in = captured at intake; AI voice script is simple (wellness summary + no concerns vs. concern flagged).

### D — VoiceGuard

**What it does:** AI voice scheduled safety check-in for lone workers — field technicians, home inspectors, insurance adjusters, solar installers, and utility workers who operate alone in potentially hazardous environments. AI makes a scheduled voice call ("Hi, this is your safety check-in — press 1 to confirm you're safe"). No response or DISTRESS keyword triggers an automated SMS alert to the designated supervisor.

**Evidence:**
- OSHA regulations (29 CFR 1910) require employers to establish procedures for lone worker safety; many states have additional requirements. Manual check-ins (supervisors calling each field tech individually) are labor-intensive and inconsistently performed.
- Market: 10M+ lone workers in the US across construction, utilities, home services, insurance, real estate inspection — large addressable market in aggregate.
- Structural barrier: buyer is a company's HR director, safety manager, or operations director — not an owner-operator making a spontaneous tool purchase. Procurement cycles at companies with 10+ field workers are measured in weeks to months, not days.
- Adjacent to rejected ShiftText (frontline teams keyword, Day ~16): ShiftText was rejected. VoiceGuard serves a distinct use case (safety compliance vs. shift scheduling) but shares the same buyer profile (company HR/operations) and the same procurement friction that blocked ShiftText.
- Reachability: ASSP (American Society of Safety Professionals), AIHA — organized but procurement-gated.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **6.5** — medium confidence. Companies do have safety compliance budgets; but procurement friction (HR/safety manager, not owner-operator) depresses sales velocity. Revenue ceiling exists per company vs. per individual buyer.
- Popularity Potential: **6.5** — medium confidence. Safety manager community is organized (ASSP, AIHA) but not viral. "AI voice safety check-in" doesn't generate peer referrals the way consumer or agent-facing products do.
