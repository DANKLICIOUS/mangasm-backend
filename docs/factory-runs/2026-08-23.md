## Candidate Scorecards

### A — TradeCompliance

**What it does:** License renewal and continuing education deadline SMS reminders for owner-operated licensed trade businesses (solo/small electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians). Tracks state-specific renewal windows and sends deadline alerts — no content delivery, no curriculum.

**Evidence:**
- Every US state with licensed trades requires periodic CE credit completion (typically 8-32 hours per 2-year cycle) and license renewal; lapsing means the contractor legally cannot operate — the penalty is immediate and severe.
- State license boards publish renewal schedules publicly; many mail a single postcard reminder. No dominant SMS-specific tool targets the solo/small contractor segment for renewal tracking.
- Reachability: trade association forums (PHCC, ACCA, NECA local chapters) and Facebook groups for licensed tradespeople are active; same outreach pattern as auto repair and landscaping.
- No content creation burden: TradeCompliance does not deliver CE courses — it only tracks deadlines and links to the contractor's own state board portal. This keeps the product simple and avoids any curriculum liability.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **7.0** — medium confidence. Contractor businesses are revenue-motivated (license lapse = zero revenue); $79/mo subscription is defensible. Market size smaller than vet or grooming (est. 600,000 licensed electricians/plumbers/HVAC in US) but concentrated in identifiable geographies. Lower urgency outside the 90-day renewal window.
- Popularity Potential: **7.0** — medium confidence. Reachable via trade association channels; word-of-mouth within local trade networks is strong. Product appeal is narrow but high within the niche — "will never miss a renewal again" is a clear message.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **7.5** — high confidence. SMS reminders only; no curriculum, no integration with licensing boards required for MVP (owner inputs their own renewal date); keyword set CONFIRM/STOP; identical engine to existing verticals.

**Composite:** (7.0 × 0.40) + (7.0 × 0.35) + (7.5 × 0.25) = 2.80 + 2.45 + 1.875 = **7.125**

**Build gate check:**
- Composite ≥ 7.0: ✅ (7.125)

### B — CompliLearn

**What it does:** OSHA 10/30 certification expiry tracking and recertification reminder SMS for construction subcontractors and site safety coordinators.

**Evidence:**
- OSHA 10/30 certifications do not expire under OSHA rules (no mandatory renewal interval set by federal OSHA); some general contractors and project owners require recertification every 3-5 years as a site access condition — this is contractual, not regulatory.
- The undefined expiry window means the trigger for the reminder is ambiguous; without a clear, universal expiration date, the reminder product has weak default cadence.
- Content liability risk: if CompliLearn sends incorrect recertification deadline information and a subcontractor fails a site access check, liability exposure rises — unlike trade licenses (public state records), OSHA recertification requirements vary per GC contract.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **7.5** — medium confidence. Construction market is large; safety compliance spend is high. Pain is real on project sites.
- Popularity Potential: **6.5** — medium confidence. Target buyer unclear (subcontractor owner vs. GC safety coordinator vs. union hall); fragmented by trade.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **6.0** — medium confidence. Ambiguous expiry windows require custom per-user input; harder to onboard than a fixed state-board deadline.

**Composite:** (7.5 × 0.40) + (6.5 × 0.35) + (6.0 × 0.25) = 3.00 + 2.275 + 1.50 = **6.775**

**Build gate check:**
- Composite ≥ 7.0: ❌ (6.775)
- **GATE: FAILED ❌** — ambiguous expiry model weakens ease of use; content liability risk elevated vs. TradeCompliance.

### C — HIPAALearn

**What it does:** HIPAA workforce training deadline reminders for small medical, dental, and behavioral health practices.

**Evidence:**
- HIPAA requires covered entities to train workforce members but does not specify a mandatory interval — OCR expects "periodic" training, typically interpreted as annual by compliance consultants.
- Dominant players: Compliancy Group, HIPAA One, MedTrainer — these are full-suite HIPAA compliance platforms that bundle training reminders as a feature, not a standalone product.
- Fatal risk: sending deadline reminder SMS into a healthcare practice context raises HIPAA BAA questions (even if the reminder is administrative, the context of the business is covered entity). Solo founder cannot absorb BAA compliance overhead at MVP stage.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **7.5** — medium confidence. Healthcare compliance spend is high; practices pay.
- Popularity Potential: **6.5** — medium confidence. Compliance consultant channel is the real buyer; direct-to-practice cold outreach is slow.

## Decision

**Winner:** TradeCompliance (License Renewal + CE Deadline Reminder SMS for Licensed Trades)  
**Composite Score:** 7.125  
**Decision:** BUILD ✅ — 10th build-gate winner  
**Why it won:** TradeCompliance is the only candidate with a hard, universal, publicly-verifiable deadline (state license renewal dates) and zero content liability — it tracks deadlines and links to the state board portal, never delivers curriculum. The immediate financial consequence of lapse (can't legally work) creates strong motivation to pay. Architecture is identical to existing SMS engine; no new code required for MVP.

**Strongest unresolved assumption:** Market concentration by trade type — electrician, plumber, and HVAC may have different renewal cycle lengths and CE credit requirements by state. An MVP must either focus on one trade type in one state, or build a state × trade lookup table before launch. Without that, onboarding requires too much manual input per contractor.
