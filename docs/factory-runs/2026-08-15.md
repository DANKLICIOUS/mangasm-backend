## Evidence Notes (sourced 2026-08-15)

- **A, B (AI-drafted-reply Pro tier):** real, sourced precedent that
  AI-feature premium tiers work as a monetization pattern in 2026
  SaaS — Notion bundles AI into higher-priced plans, Canva raised Teams
  pricing up to 300% explicitly citing AI feature expansion, and tiered
  pricing is the standard way SaaS companies "capture willingness to pay
  at the top" while containing variable AI-compute cost on the base tier
  ([news.aakashg.com](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/how-to-price-ai-products),
  [getmonetizely.com](https://www.getmonetizely.com/blogs/the-2026-guide-to-saas-ai-and-agentic-pricing-models)).
  This is general SaaS-market evidence, not specific to trades/appointment
  SMB buyers, so popularity confidence stays low — but it's a real,
  current pattern worth having on record, and it maps directly onto the
  optional paid AI-fallback path already designed (not yet built or
  priced) in the shared engine's build prompt.
- **C (PawText usage-based):** no fresh search this run; carries forward
  the repeatedly-confirmed smaller-ticket-size finding for the pet-care
  vertical (07-30, 08-01, 08-04, 08-05) at low confidence for this
  specific pricing-model question.
- **D (LoneCheck subscription):** the pricing-model switch doesn't touch
  the actual reason this candidate failed on 2026-08-05 — SMS safety
  check-ins are already a bundled feature of full lone-worker-safety
  platforms, which is a buyer-clarity problem, not a pricing problem.
  `buyer_clear` correctly stays false regardless of pricing model, closing
  this pain out definitively across both pricing dimensions now tested.
