## Candidate Scorecards

### A — RecoveryText

**What it does:** Missed-call text-back and accountability check-in reminder SMS for independent sober living home operators and recovery coaching businesses. Missed inquiry call from a prospective resident or family member triggers an immediate SMS follow-up; structured reminder sequence (24hr + 1hr) drives check-in appointment confirmation for active residents.

**Evidence:**
- Sober living homes are private-pay businesses: residents pay $1,500-3,000/month for housing and structured accountability. The operator (house manager or owner) runs the facility as a business with real revenue.
- Core pain: Residents miss mandatory accountability check-ins, skip confirmation calls for required community meetings, and forget house rule appointments — no-shows create compliance risk (courts, parole officers) and churn risk for the operator.
- Current tooling gap: Operators use manual SMS group chats and individual texts — no purpose-built missed-call + structured check-in reminder SMS tool exists for the recovery housing niche.
- TCPA: Residents sign detailed intake forms at move-in that already include communication consent — opt-in documentation is a built-in part of standard intake, simpler than cold-prospect contexts.
- Market size: 14,000+ sober living homes certified or affiliated with NARR (National Alliance for Recovery Residences) in the US; recovery coaching separately is a growing ~$1B paid-coaching market with 5,000+ independent practitioners.
- Reachability: NARR member directory, Sober Living Network, recovery house owner Facebook groups (Sober Living Operators community), regional recovery housing coalitions — all active, organized peer channels.
- Dual trigger fit: (1) missed_call from prospective resident or family member inquiring about bed availability; (2) reminder_due for daily or weekly accountability check-in, mandatory meeting confirmation (same architecture as VetText).

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **7.5** — medium/high confidence. $99-149/mo for a 10-resident facility is trivial relative to $15,000-30,000 monthly revenue; single retained resident far exceeds annual subscription cost. 14,000+ facilities nationally; recovery coaching segment adds 5,000+ independent buyers. No dominant purpose-built SMS competitor identified.
- Popularity Potential: **7.5** — medium confidence. Recovery community is tight-knit with strong word-of-mouth; NARR network provides organized, directory-accessible reachability; growing media and policy attention on recovery housing creates favorable market timing for operator-tool adoption.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **7.5** — high confidence. Check-in = appointment; missed inquiry = standard missed_call trigger; CONFIRM/CHECK-IN/STOP keywords; no clinical integration required (accountability housing, not clinical treatment); onboard in under 10 minutes.

**Composite:** (7.5 × 0.40) + (7.5 × 0.35) + (7.5 × 0.25) = 3.000 + 2.625 + 1.875 = **7.500**

### B — NeuroText

**What it does:** Missed-call text-back and session reminder SMS for independent ADHD and neurodiversity coaching practices. Inquiry call from a prospective client triggers immediate SMS follow-up; 24hr + 1hr reminder sequence drives coaching session confirmation and reduces the ironic no-show problem inherent in serving ADHD clients.

**Evidence:**
- ADHD coaching is a growing, private-pay coaching market — coaches are not licensed clinicians, so no HIPAA applies. Coaches charge $150-350/session; clients frequently miss sessions due to executive function challenges (the core symptom being treated), making SMS reminders uniquely high-value in this niche.
- Current tools: most ADHD coaches use Calendly or Acuity for scheduling — both send email confirmations only; SMS reminder gap is real and specifically painful given the client population.
- Market: ~4,000 PAAC-certified ADHD coaches in the US; 10,000+ ADHD-informed executive function coaches and neurodiversity tutors who operate private coaching practices.
- Reachability: PAAC (Professional Association of ADHD Coaches), ICF ADHD Coaching specialty group, CHADD (Children and Adults with ADHD) professional network — organized and accessible.
- Dual trigger fit: (1) missed_call from prospective client inquiring about coaching packages; (2) reminder_due for upcoming session with CONFIRM/RESCHEDULE/STOP keywords.
- Distinction from CareText (care coordination): ADHD coaches are not caregivers or home health aides — this is a distinct professional coaching segment with a different buyer profile (independent practitioner vs. care agency operator).

- Ease of Use/Learning: **8.0** — high confidence. Session = appointment; CONFIRM/RESCHEDULE/STOP keywords; missed_call trigger for inquiry; no clinical integration; architecture is identical to VetText and StudioText; onboard under 10 minutes.

### C — AccessText

**What it does:** Activity confirmation and transport reminder SMS for independent disability community access programs — day programs and supported-employment programs serving adults with developmental disabilities, primarily Medicaid waiver-funded operators.

**Evidence:**
- Community access and day programs for adults with developmental disabilities are scheduled daily/weekly services; transportation no-shows and activity cancellations are a significant operational cost.
- Some programs are for-profit operators under state Medicaid waiver contracts (Home and Community-Based Services); others are nonprofits. The for-profit Medicaid waiver operator segment is growing as states shift to managed care models.
- Pain is real: a transport no-show cascades — driver dispatched, staff scheduled, day program slot wasted.
- The critical structural barrier: most operators are reimbursed by Medicaid at fixed daily rates ($85-120/day per participant). Margins are thin and controlled by state reimbursement rates, not market pricing. Technology spend approval often requires supervisory sign-off; sales cycles are long.
- Pricing sensitivity: $99/mo is affordable in absolute terms but Medicaid-funded programs treat software purchases as overhead subject to reimbursement scrutiny. Decision-maker is often a program director, not an owner.

**Scores (raw, before weighting):**
- Profit Potential: **6.5** — medium confidence. For-profit Medicaid waiver operators could pay; nonprofit operators are budget-constrained by reimbursement rates. Payment decision is slow (overhead approval), not spontaneous. Revenue ceiling exists.
- Popularity Potential: **7.0** — medium confidence. Word-of-mouth within disability services networks (NADSP, ANCOR) is organized; providers are reachable. But procurement friction dampens viral adoption compared to independent business owners.
- Ease of Use/Learning: **7.5** — high confidence. Activity = appointment; transport confirmation = standard reminder; CONFIRM/CANCEL/STOP keywords; no clinical integration needed; architecture identical to prior winners.

**Composite:** (6.5 × 0.40) + (7.0 × 0.35) + (7.5 × 0.25) = 2.600 + 2.450 + 1.875 = **6.925**

- Composite ≥ 7.0: ❌ (6.925 — near-miss)

- buyer_clear: ✅ (for-profit Medicaid waiver day program operator)

## Pattern Notes

"Inclusive community" keyword produced two build-gate passers (RecoveryText, NeuroText) — the strongest same-day dual-pass result since the local business sweep. Common thread: both winners serve private-pay B2B operators or independent practitioners in growing markets where the client population itself (recovery residents with accountability needs; ADHD clients with executive function challenges) makes SMS reminders disproportionately high-value. New pattern from this run: **community-of-care businesses** (sober living, neurodiversity coaching) outperform traditional nonprofit "inclusive community" organizations when evaluated against the SMS engine's B2B SaaS model. The "inclusive" positioning of the winner is a go-to-market differentiator, not a structural handicap.
