## Evidence Notes (sourced 2026-08-20)

- **A (FitText):** solid pain evidence, similar shape to 08-19's SalonText.
  SMS reminders reduce class/session no-shows by roughly 20-40% (38% in one
  cited study), SMS open rates run ~98% vs 20-35% for email, and studios
  using SMS automation for confirmations/reminders/recovery report class
  fill rates climbing from 40-50% to 67% capacity
  ([wellnessliving.com](https://www.wellnessliving.com/blog/stop-no-shows-proven-tactics-for-fitness-studios/),
  [textmunication.com](https://www.textmunication.com/blog/fitness-studio-sms-marketing)).
  The gate failure is the same shape as SalonText's: fitness/gym booking
  software is a mature, consolidated category (Mindbody, Glofox, Zen
  Planner, WellnessLiving) where SMS reminders typically ship bundled into
  the full booking/membership platform rather than being bought as a
  standalone text-back tool — a second confirmation of 08-19's "reminder
  functionality bundled into a dominant booking incumbent" failure pattern,
  this time in a different vertical.
- **B (CleanText) — the strongest quantified pain since ShopText.** Without
  reminders, cleaning businesses commonly run 15-20% no-shows/missed visits;
  at roughly $150 per missed visit, just 4 no-shows a month costs $600+,
  while a structured SMS reminder cadence (confirm at booking → 48h
  reminder → 2h reminder) brings that down to 2-5%
  ([miocommerce.com](https://miocommerce.com/blog/how-automated-sms-reminders-cut-no-show-rates-for-service-businesses),
  [leadduo.io](https://www.leadduo.io/en/blog/automated-reminders-cleaning-appointments)).
  Missed-call text-back specifically is framed as the dividing line between
  cleaning companies that convert inbound inquiries and those that don't
  ("what separates the 20% of cleaning companies that consistently convert
  inbound inquiries from the 80% that do not is a single piece of
  infrastructure: automated text-back") — a buyer-clarity signal as strong
  as TextBack Local's original trades finding
  ([ustechautomations.com](https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-best-missed-call-textback-software-for-cleaning-companies-2026)).
  Competitive landscape check: dedicated missed-call/reminder tools already
  exist for cleaning companies (unlike fitness, where the feature lives
  inside dominant all-in-one suites), but the space stays fragmented —
  small, cleaning-specific vendors, not a single Mindbody/Booksy-class
  incumbent — the same fragmentation pattern that let ShopText clear the
  gate in auto repair on 08-19.
- **C, D (usage-based variants):** no fresh pricing-switch evidence sourced
  for either. Fitness class schedules run on a steady weekly/monthly
  membership rhythm, the same logic that has failed usage-based pricing for
  every steady-cadence local-business winner so far (NoShowGuard 08-14,
  SalonText 08-19). Cleaning visit frequency is more genuinely mixed
  (one-time deep cleans vs. twice-weekly recurring clients), a real,
  honest hypothesis paralleling ShopText usage-based's irregular-volume
  case — but it isn't backed by fresh evidence this run, so it stays low
  confidence and correctly fails the gate on that basis alone regardless of
  composite.

## Winner — CleanText · verdict: BUILD ✅ (composite 7.48, all 6 gates passed)

**Concept:** SMS-based missed-call text-back combined with visit-day
confirmation and reminder texts for independent and small residential
house-cleaning companies — recovers a lost booking inquiry the moment a
call goes unanswered, then follows up with a 48-hour and 2-hour reminder
sequence to cut the no-show rate on booked visits. Same core SMS engine and
architecture as the other 6 winners (`docs/build-prompts/sms-core-engine.md`
applies directly).

**This is the 7th build-gate winner overall**, and its composite (7.48) is
the second-highest of any winner to date, behind only RezText (7.65) and
above TextBack Local (7.40). Its evidence combines the two strongest
patterns seen separately in prior runs: a directly quantified dollar-loss
figure from missed visits (like ShopText's $400/repair-order finding) and a
sharp buyer-clarity signal on missed-call text-back specifically as the
differentiator between businesses that convert and businesses that don't
(echoing TextBack Local's original trades thesis). Meanwhile FitText
confirms, for the second day running, that reminder functionality bundled
into a dominant all-in-one booking incumbent (Mindbody/Glofox for gyms, as
Booksy/Vagaro/Squire were for salons on 08-19) is a reliable way for an
otherwise well-evidenced candidate to fail on popularity alone.
