## Evidence Notes (sourced 2026-08-18)

- **A (RezText) — the strongest evidence pull since 2026-08-02.**
  **67% of restaurant reservations are still made by phone**, making missed-
  call exposure structurally larger than any vertical tested to date.
  Restaurants sending a **3-touch SMS sequence see no-show rates drop from
  20-25% down to 5-8%**; SMS confirmations and reminders report no-show
  reductions of **up to 38%**; and **89% of U.S. consumers have opted in**
  to receive texts from at least one business
  ([blaksheepcreative.com](https://blaksheepcreative.com/missed-calls-for-restaurants/),
  [restaurantsnapshotforghl.com](https://restaurantsnapshotforghl.com/blog/restaurant-missed-call-text-back/),
  [ai-agentsplus.com](https://www.ai-agentsplus.com/blog/-missed-call-text-back-never-lose-a-restaurant-lead-again/)).
  Unlike prior local-business candidates, restaurants have **both** the
  missed-call pain (TextBack Local's mechanism) and the no-show pain
  (NoShowGuard's mechanism) as one natural combined product, not an
  artificial cross-sell bundle — the sourced material itself describes an
  integrated missed-call-to-confirmation-to-reminder flow, not two separate
  tools. Real competitive landscape exists (Bite Buddy AI, restaurant-
  specific missed-call agencies) but is less saturated than trades' AI-
  voice-receptionist category (2026-08-15's CallCatch Local refresh).
- **B (RezText usage-based):** no fresh evidence for the pricing switch
  specifically; restaurants generally have predictable weekly reservation
  patterns (Fri/Sat peaks, but consistent week over week), which is the
  classic case for flat subscription over usage-based — the same
  conclusion reached for NoShowGuard on 2026-08-14, now replicated in a new
  vertical.
- **C (Retail Recovery):** real, decision-grade evidence, comparable in
  strength to TextBack Local's original pain case. **85% of consumers who
  call a small business and get no answer will not call back**; small
  businesses miss **20-35% of calls during business hours and nearly 100%
  after hours**; automatic text-back recovers **25-45% of missed callers**
  ([lenochat.com](https://www.lenochat.com/blog/most-small-stores-dont-lose-sales-because-of-price-they-lose-them-because-no-one-answers),
  [dialraven.com](https://dialraven.com/blog/missed-call-text-back-for-small-business/)).
  Falls just short of the gate (6.90) primarily on popularity — the
  competitive field includes ecommerce-focused tools (Ringly) and AI phone
  agents already addressing this exact pain.
- **D (RezText Catering):** no fresh search this run; catering/private-
  event leads are higher-ticket but lower-frequency than table
  reservations, and the narrower buyer segment (restaurants with a
  meaningful catering line, not all restaurants) pulls popularity down
  without matching evidence to support a higher profit score. Carried at
  low confidence.

## Winner — RezText · verdict: BUILD ✅ (composite 7.65, all 6 gates passed)

**Concept:** SMS-based missed-call text-back combined with reservation
confirmation and reminder sequencing for independent restaurants —
recovers lost reservation and catering calls the moment they're missed,
then follows up with a 3-touch confirm/remind sequence before the booked
time to cut no-shows. Same core SMS engine and architecture as the
existing 4 winners (`docs/build-prompts/sms-core-engine.md` applies
directly — new vertical config, not new infrastructure).

**This is the 5th build-gate winner overall and the highest composite score
of any candidate scored to date (7.65, beating TextBack Local's 7.40).**
It didn't come from re-slicing the exhausted trades/appointment space — it
came from testing a genuinely different buyer (restaurants) with
structurally larger call-volume exposure (67% phone-based reservations)
and a naturally combined missed-call-plus-no-show product, rather than two
separate tools requiring a cross-sell.
