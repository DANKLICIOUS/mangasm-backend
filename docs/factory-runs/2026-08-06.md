## Evidence Notes (sourced 2026-08-06)

- **A (ShiftText Compliance):** strong evidence, but it cuts against the
  candidate. SMS-delivered microlearning drives completion rates **above
  95%**, compared with typical LMS engagement **below 30%**
  ([qstream.com](https://qstream.com/qstream-drives-fast-learning-engagement-via-sms/)).
  The problem: this is not a gap in the market, it's an already-solved one —
  named platforms (**Arist**, **Qstream**) already deliver SMS/WhatsApp/Teams
  compliance microlearning at scale with exactly this completion-rate
  advantage as their core pitch
  ([arist.com](https://arist.com/resources/blogs/personal-device-compliant-training-platforms)).
  Popularity scored down accordingly — the pain is real and the delivery
  mechanism is validated, but this specific product would be entering a
  category with funded incumbents already owning the same mechanism
  advantage, not discovering it.
- **D (VerifyText Local):** real counter-signal, the most useful finding of
  the run. Homeowner-vetting search results surfaced active scam campaigns
  that impersonate official contractor-license verification via text
  message, and licensing boards explicitly warn consumers that **official
  agencies will never contact licensees via text for this purpose**
  ([cslb.ca.gov](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Resources/IndustryBulletins/2022/Fraud_Alert_Dec_2022.pdf),
  [bdrco.com](https://www.bdrco.com/blog/fake-compliance-notices-scam/)).
  A legitimate SMS-based contractor-verification product would be launching
  directly into a channel homeowners are actively being trained to distrust
  — the delivery mechanism that wins for reminders and confirmations
  actively works against a product whose entire value proposition is
  establishing trust. `fatal_risk` marked true and `buyer_clear` marked
  false: no clean way to differentiate a legitimate verification text from
  the scam pattern without the very institutional backing (state licensing
  boards) this product doesn't have.
- **B (PawVerify):** no fresh search this run; adjacent to pet-scam
  precedent from general knowledge (online puppy-sale fraud is a documented
  problem) but no sourced pricing or demand evidence gathered today, and the
  buyer is unclear — pet owners themselves have no obvious willingness to
  pay for a verification tool mid-transaction versus simply using an
  established marketplace with its own vetting. `buyer_clear` marked false.
- **C (ComplyText Community):** no fresh search this run; carries forward
  the general compliance-training-via-SMS completion-rate evidence from
  candidate A at a discount, since the buyer here (nonprofit/community-org
  operations staff managing volunteer background-check renewals) has a much
  thinner training budget than a for-profit frontline employer. No conflict-
  of-interest flag this time — this is internal ops tooling, not a
  community/social feature, so it doesn't duplicate Mangasm's own product
  surface the way past "inclusive community + safe connection" candidates
  did.

## Winner — ShiftText Compliance · verdict: VALIDATE (composite 6.53, gate NOT passed)

**Concept:** SMS-delivered microlearning for mandatory, recurring compliance
training (safety, food handling, harassment prevention) aimed at frontline
and deskless employers. The completion-rate evidence is genuinely strong —
stronger than any single evidence pull in this run — but the category
already has funded, proven incumbents (Arist, Qstream) executing the exact
same mechanism advantage at scale. This fails the build gate on
`composite_at_least_7` and `no_category_below_6` (popularity 5.5), not on
weak evidence — it's the rare case where the evidence is strong and the
conclusion is still "don't build this specific thing," because the gap it
would fill is already filled.

**This is the first run since 2026-08-01 with no build-gate winner.** Eight
runs in, 36 candidates generated, 4 have now cleared the gate (all SMS,
covering trades/trucking/appointments) — today's result is a useful data
point that SMS-as-delivery is not a universal edge; it wins specifically
where the underlying job is currently handled by phone or nothing, not
everywhere text messaging could technically apply.
