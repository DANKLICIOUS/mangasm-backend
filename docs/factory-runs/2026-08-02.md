## Winner — TextBack Local · verdict: BUILD ✅ (composite 7.40, all 6 gates passed)

**Concept:** SMS-first missed-call recovery for local service businesses —
when a call goes unanswered, an automatic text goes out within seconds
("Sorry we missed you — what can we help with?"), capturing the lead and
routing it into the existing booking/bid workflow. Same buyer as 07-28's
CallCatch Local (home-services owner-operator), same purchase trigger (missed
calls = lost jobs), but SMS instead of voice — simpler build, same evidence
base, sourced $30-300/mo pricing precedent.

**This is now the SECOND concept to clear the build gate** (after CallCatch
Local, 7.08). Both target the identical pain/market; the difference is pure
delivery mechanism — and SMS scored *higher* (7.40 vs 7.08), driven almost
entirely by the ease advantage of not needing voice/telephony infrastructure.

## Portfolio recommendation — this changes the build order

Six runs in, the pattern is now a decision, not a trend: **the Ecosystem
Builder's first shipped product should be SMS text-back, not the voice AI
receptionist.** Same validated pain, same buyer, cheaper and faster to build
and to demo (a `generate-ecosystem.mjs`-style SMS auto-reply is a much
smaller surface than a voice pipeline). Voice AI remains the *upsell* path
once text-back proves out with a paying customer.
