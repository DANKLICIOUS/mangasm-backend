## Evidence Notes (sourced 2026-08-19)

- **A (SalonText):** solid pain evidence. Hair salons and barbershops
  average a **15-25% baseline no-show rate**; automated reminders bring
  that under 10%; a real barbershop's 3-touch system (booking confirm,
  24h reminder, 2h reminder) dropped no-shows to **6% within 30 days**
  ([agentzap.ai](https://agentzap.ai/blog/how-to-reduce-barbershop-no-shows-by-70-proven-strategies-for-barbers),
  [etisia.com](https://www.etisia.com/no-show-statistics)). The gate
  failure is on popularity: salon/barbershop booking software is a mature,
  consolidated SaaS category (Booksy, Vagaro, Squire) where reminder
  functionality typically ships bundled into the full booking platform
  rather than being bought standalone — a tighter competitive squeeze than
  restaurants, where the competing tools found were smaller and more
  fragmented.
- **B (ShopText) — the strongest quantified pain of any run to date.**
  The average auto repair shop **misses 30% of incoming calls, climbing to
  40-50% during busy periods**; at a **$400 average repair order** and 50
  calls/week, a 30% miss rate is **15 lost opportunities weekly** — a
  directly quantified revenue-loss figure, not just a rate
  ([mysmsgate.net](https://mysmsgate.net/en/blog/sms-for-auto-repair-shops),
  [skipcalls.com](https://skipcalls.com/auto-repair/how-do-i-stop-missing-calls-at-my-auto-shop-when-the-lifts-are-running-and-my-ha)).
  Competitive landscape exists (SkipCalls, Eligant, MySMSGate) but is more
  fragmented than salon booking software — no single incumbent dominates
  auto-repair-shop communication tools the way Booksy/Vagaro do for
  salons.
- **C, D (usage-based variants):** no fresh evidence for either pricing
  switch specifically. Salon booking rhythm is steady and weekly, the
  classic case for flat subscription (same reasoning as NoShowGuard,
  2026-08-14). Auto repair volume is comparatively more irregular
  week-to-week, which is the same logic that favored usage-based for
  SMSDispatch's trucking buyer — a real, honest hypothesis, but it isn't
  backed by fresh evidence this run, so it stays low confidence and
  correctly fails the gate on that basis alone despite a high composite.

## Winner — ShopText · verdict: BUILD ✅ (composite 7.30, all 6 gates passed)

**Concept:** SMS-based missed-call text-back combined with service-status
and vehicle-pickup reminders for independent auto repair shops — recovers
lost repair-order leads the moment a call is missed, then follows up with
status/pickup texts to reduce no-shows on completed work. Same core SMS
engine and architecture as the other 5 winners
(`docs/build-prompts/sms-core-engine.md` applies directly).

**This is the 6th build-gate winner overall.** Its pain evidence is the
most concretely quantified of any run to date — a directly stated dollar
figure (15 lost $400 repair orders/week from missed calls alone) rather
than a percentage requiring translation into revenue impact. Sits third by
composite among the six winners (below RezText 7.65 and TextBack Local
7.40, above SMSDispatch/NoShowGuard 7.28 and CallCatch Local 7.08).
